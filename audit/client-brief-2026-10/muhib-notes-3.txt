About page: I am an English teacher, writer, and voice artist with 12 years of classroom experience across four countries. My work is grounded in listening closely, identifying what is needed and responding with care. Discovering that I had a natural knack with the English language from a young age, I then knew that I wanted to use these same words to help others; whether teaching, writing, or performing them. Words have elevated people by allowing them to understand not only themselves, but the world around them, and have granted them real opportunities in their own lives. My teenage years and early twenties were spent at various acting schools across the Midlands, culminating in my training at the renowned Television Workshop, where I became familiar with auditioning for both plays and casting directors. This fed into my already existent love for theatre and film. During this time, I also performed as a spoken word poet and volunteered my time reading stories in care homes, where I further indulged in my love for performing words and bringing them to life. From a young age I also knew I wanted to teach abroad and see the world. So upon graduating with a 1st class in English, I initially taught as an English teaching assistant across primary and secondary schools, before teaching English as a second language with the British Council in Egypt, before then moving to Saudi Arabia. I returned to the UK to gain my PGCE before receiving my QTS in Qatar. It was here that I then taught English Language and Literature to 11-18 year olds for 5 years at a private British international school. From America to Australia; Canada to Croatia; or Morocco to Malaysia - my extensive travels have only ever deep-rooted love for words, culture, and connection. After years of travel, a textured word is a place where my love for words, literature, analysis and performance can finally come and take root; where I can encapsulate my years wrapped up in all things - and use these skills to help others achieve their own goals; whether for an exam, content, or an audiobook. It is a place that enables me to work with an array of people, all with different goals in mind, but a shared interest in utilising words to get there.

Voice: Further details Back in 2015, when I set up my first voiceover business, Stylish Voice, I had my professional demos recorded with Gary Terzza; channel 4’s continuity host for over two decades. It was under his tutelage that I made my first foray into the voiceover world and began to understand exactly what goes into bringing the very best of your voice into work that eventually spanned across audiobooks, commercials, explainers, IVRs, and much more.
